What is risky?

Inverse bonding curves for Robinhood Chain memecoins

/mint

The curve runs backwards

Minting pushes the price down. Burning pushes it back up. The opposite of every market you know.

minting → price down 1.0000
/leverage

Leverage on the memes

Mint against a coin's reserve and your position moves at a multiple of spot. No order book, no counterparty.

spot +0.0% +0.0%
/markets

Every coin on Robinhood Chain

AI, MEME, GRASS, BONER, MOO, SHROOM and the rest. Anyone can open a market for any token. No permission needed.

/stake

Fees land on stakers

Every mint, burn and LP move pays a 0.5% fee. Stake your ibAssets and take your share of all of it.

Buy it down. Sell it up.

Price falls as ibAssets are minted and rises as they are burned. The reserve never leaves the curve.
